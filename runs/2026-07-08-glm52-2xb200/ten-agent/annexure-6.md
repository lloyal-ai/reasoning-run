## Japan's Elderly-Care Equipment & Assistive/Mobility Devices Market (Segment 6)

### Headline Market Size (JASPA Welfare Equipment Survey)

- **FY2021 welfare equipment (福祉用具) market size: ¥1,521.4 billion (¥1.52 trillion)**, up **2.9% year-on-year** (102.9% of FY2020).
- Announced by JASPA (日本福祉用具・生活支援用具協会) on September 15, 2023.
- The survey is the standard industry-wide sizing of Japan's welfare equipment market; methodology lineage traces to a METI survey started in FY1993, taken over by JASPA from FY2003 (manufacturer questionnaire + government/industry statistics + hearings).
